    How Savvy uses your data →

    A few things to know.

    Do I have to connect my bank?

    No. Savvy works from the names of the cards you carry. No bank login required.

    Can I look before creating an account?

    Yes. Pick your cards and Savvy shows what they include right away. You add your phone number only when you want Savvy to keep watching them.

    How does Savvy know what I've used?

    Only what you tell it. Reply "Done" to a text, mark a credit used in Savvy, or forward the receipt, and it stays out of your reminders until it resets. Your issuer statement is the final word on what has posted.

    What does it cost?

    Trial reminders are free: forward a free-trial email and Savvy reminds you before it charges, with or without a membership. Watching your cards' credits, the texts and your AI assistant are free for 30 days, then $19.99 a month. One missed $100 credit covers five months.

    Does Savvy spend money or cancel things for me?

    No. It reminds you and answers questions. You make the purchase or the cancellation.

    Which cards does Savvy know?

    2,600+ benefits across 270+ cards, from the big premium cards to airline and hotel co-brands. Type yours into the search above to check.
