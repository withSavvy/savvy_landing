01

The Program

Savvy operates one text-message program: Savvy Account Notifications & Benefit Reminders. It sends recurring reminders before card benefits and statement credits expire, plus confirmations tied to your own account activity.

  • Program name: Savvy Account Notifications & Benefit Reminders
  • Operated by: Sabera Daqiq, a sole proprietorship doing business as Savvy
  • Message frequency: varies, typically about weekly
  • Cost: Savvy does not charge for texts. Message and data rates may apply from your carrier.
  • Audience: existing, signed-in Savvy users who have opted in themselves

This page exists so anyone — a user, a carrier, or a reviewer — can see exactly how consent is collected, in the same words the user sees.

02

How You Opt In

Consent is collected in one place only: the Notification Settings screen inside the authenticated Savvy web app at withsavvy.ai. It is never collected over the phone, in person, on paper, or from any purchased or third-party list. We never text a number we obtained from anywhere other than its own owner, inside their signed-in account.

Consent is given by tapping a button labelled “Turn On Texts”, with the full disclosure shown directly above that button. The tap is the affirmative act — there is no checkbox anywhere in the flow, so there is nothing that could be pre-selected, and nothing can be submitted on your behalf. Texts stay off until you tap it.

Which steps you see depends on whether your mobile number was already verified when you created your account:

  • Step 1. You create a Savvy account and sign in.
  • Step 2. You open Notification Settings.
  • Step 3a. If your number was already verified at sign-up: that number is shown to you, the disclosure appears beneath it, and you tap Turn On Texts. Possession was already proven when the number was verified, so no further step is needed.
  • Step 3b. If it was not: you type your own mobile number, the disclosure appears above the button, and you tap Turn On Texts. You then confirm possession from the device itself before any message is sent — see below.
  • Step 4. Your consent is stored against your account with a timestamp.

04

Proving the Number Is Yours

Tapping the button is not enough on its own. Before Savvy sends a single message, the number must be proven to belong to you. If it was already verified when you created your account, that proof is what makes the one-tap path available. Otherwise you prove it from the device in your hand, by either:

  • texting START or VERIFY to the Savvy number from that device, or
  • entering a one-time code that we send to that number.

Savvy never sends reminders to a number that has not completed this step. This protects someone whose number is entered by mistake — or by anyone else.

05

What We Send

Every message identifies Savvy as the sender and relates to your own account activity. Representative examples:

Savvy: Your $50 Amex Gold dining credit expires in 3 days — don't lose it. Reply DONE when used, SNOOZE to skip this week, STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help.
Savvy: Marked your $15 Uber Cash credit as used. Nice. Reply STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help.

We do not send marketing or promotional messages, and we never message you about anything unrelated to your Savvy account.

06

STOP, HELP, and Other Keywords

Every keyword below works at any time, on any message.

ReplyWhat happens
STOPYou are unsubscribed immediately and we send one confirmation. No further messages are sent.
HELPWe reply with program information and our support contact.
START / VERIFYConfirms possession of the number after you have opted in inside the app.
DONEMarks the benefit in the reminder as used.
SNOOZESkips reminders for a week.

UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, and QUIT are honored the same way as STOP. You can also switch the setting off in the app at any time, which has the same effect.

Your Opt-In Data Is Never Sold or Shared

Your mobile phone number, your SMS consent, and your text-messaging opt-in data are never sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. They are used only to deliver the messages you asked for.

Full detail on collection, retention, and deletion is in our Privacy & Trust page; the messaging terms are in our Terms of Service.
